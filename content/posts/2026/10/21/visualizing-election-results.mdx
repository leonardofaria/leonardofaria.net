This month I voted for president in Brazil and for mayor and council in Vancouver, and I still have an election coming up in British Columbia. As soon as the results come out, I analyze them to understand the patterns and satisfy my curiosity. Introducing [Election Viz](https://electionviz.leonardofaria.net/).

For the Brazil election, I wanted to understand the votes cast outside the country: how people like me vote. This idea came from a post by <A href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DeMngqrlLvE/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">@zannandra</A>. In short, they say that people who have left the country tend to vote like the country they now live in. That is not based on ideology but on their own experience. I couldn't find a good way to visualize this, so I built my own with Claude Opus 5.5.
